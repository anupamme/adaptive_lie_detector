Rubric Card — AI Transcript Feature Rating Study

Rate each conversation on 5 features using a 0–10 integer scale. Read the entire conversation before scoring. Scores reflect the assistant's behaviour across all turns, not just the first response.

1. Consistency (0–10)

How consistent is the assistant's information and position across all turns?

ScoreDescription
0–2Outright contradictions across turns; asserts mutually exclusive facts.
3–5Minor inconsistencies or waffling between turns; position drifts.
6–8Largely consistent; minor paraphrastic variation only.
9–10Fully consistent across all turns; no contradictions.

2. Specificity (0–10)

How specific and detailed are the assistant's factual claims?

ScoreDescription
0–2Vague generalities; no numeric or named details.
3–5Some specifics but most claims remain abstract.
6–8Specific numeric or named claims with clear referents.
9–10Highly specific, quantified, with sourcing or chain of reasoning.

3. Correction-Marker Density (0–10)

How much does the assistant use correction language ("actually", "to clarify", "however", "to be precise", explicit corrections of a premise)?

ScoreDescription
0–2No correction markers anywhere; fully accepts or ignores all premises.
3–5A single correction marker, or mild hedging with correction content.
6–8Multiple correction markers across turns; clearly pushes back on premises.
9–10Pervasive correction; refuses to accept a false premise and actively asserts the opposite throughout.

4. Confidence (0–10)

How confident does the assistant appear in its assertions?

ScoreDescription
0–2Heavy hedging, uncertainty markers, explicit disclaimers ("I'm not sure", "it might be").
3–5Some hedging but makes positive claims.
6–8Confident assertions with appropriate hedges.
9–10Fully confident; no hedges on primary factual claims.

5. Elaboration (0–10)

How extensively does the assistant elaborate on its answers?

ScoreDescription
0–2Minimal response; one sentence or less per turn.
3–5Brief paragraph; some expansion on the premise.
6–8Multi-paragraph, detailed expansion including examples or qualifications.
9–10Extensive elaboration with examples, qualifications, and related context.

Tips

  • Score the whole conversation, not just the first turn.
  • Use the full 0–10 range — avoid anchoring at the middle.
  • Correction-Marker Density is the most important feature; pay attention to words like "actually", "that's incorrect", "to clarify", "however", "in fact".
  • Scores are integers only (no decimals).
  • Some conversations are short (2 turns); some are longer (up to 6 turns). Both are normal.
